About Us

Crafting premium furniture and home solutions.

Rasluck Furniture combines skilled woodworking, modern appliance sourcing, and solar expertise to deliver beautiful, reliable solutions for homes and offices across Nigeria.

Our Story

Built on craftsmanship, driven by passion.

What started as a small workshop has grown into one of Nigeria's trusted providers of custom furniture, home appliances, and solar solutions. We have refined our craft — learning from every project, investing in better tools and materials, and building lasting relationships with homeowners and businesses across the country.

Today, Rasluck Furniture serves clients from Lagos to every corner of Nigeria, delivering bedrooms, offices, kitchens, bathrooms, and commercial spaces that reflect premium quality and personal style.
